Q1) Small Astrology Based on Life Path Number
---------------------------------------------
File: Q1.sh

Script:
echo "Enter your Birth date: ";
read date;
a=$(($date % 10))
b=$(($date / 10))
c=$(($a + $b))
case $c in
1) echo "Lucky";;
2) echo "Careful, do your works";;
3) echo "Stronger";;
4) echo "Happy";;
5) echo "Can get help";;
6) echo "Doubt";;
7) echo "Sad";;
8) echo "Like";;
9) echo "Courage";;
*) echo "Invalid life path number";;
esac

Run:
chmod 777 Q1.sh  
./Q1.sh

Output Example:
Enter your Birth date:  
23  
Life path number: 5  
Can get help

Q2) Sum and Multiplication of List of Numbers Using for Loop
-------------------------------------------------------------
File: Q2.sh

Script:
numbers=(1 2 3 4 5)
sum=0
mul=1
for num in "${numbers[@]}"
do
  sum=$((sum + num))
  mul=$((mul * num))
done
echo "Numbers: ${numbers[*]}"
echo "Summation = $sum"
echo "Multiplication = $mul"

Run:
chmod 777 Q2.sh  
./Q2.sh

Output:
Numbers: 1 2 3 4 5  
Summation = 15  
Multiplication = 120

Q3) Print Integers from 1 to 10 Using While Loop
------------------------------------------------
File: Q3.sh

Script:
num=1
while [ $num -le 10 ]
do
  echo "$num"
  num=$((num+1))
done

Run:
chmod 777 Q3.sh  
./Q3.sh

Output:
1  
2  
3  
4  
5  
6  
7  
8  
9

Q4) Print the Following Patterns
--------------------------------
File: Q4.sh

a) Increasing Pattern (Rows = 7, Columns = 7)

Script:
for((i=1; i<=7; i++))
do
  for((j=0; j<i; j++))
  do
    echo -n "* "
  done
  echo ""
done

Output:
*  
* *  
* * *  
* * * *  
* * * * *  
* * * * * *  
* * * * * * *  

------------------------------------------------------------

b) Decreasing Pattern

Script:
for((i=1; i<=7; i++))
do
  for((j=7; j>=i; j--))
  do
    echo -n "* "
  done
  echo ""
done
